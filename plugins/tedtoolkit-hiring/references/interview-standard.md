# Interview Standard

Use this standard only for one selected company role application. Apply
[hiring-integrity.md](hiring-integrity.md) before using it.

## Coverage and evidence

- Allocate time and question volume by the selected role's highest-value responsibilities,
  qualifications, and material evidence gaps.
- Use only the selected application evidence. Cite the requirement or candidate claim behind every
  primary question; never read or compare a sibling candidate.
- Balance past behavior, technical or domain depth, situational judgment, contribution ownership,
  and role motivation only when each is job-related.
- Treat missing evidence as `Not demonstrated`, never as proof of inability.
- Avoid trivia, riddles, duplicate questions, leading questions, personality proxies, and generic
  prompts that do not affect evidence for the selected role.

## Question quality and scoring anchors

Every primary question identifies the competency or source requirement, neutral follow-up probes,
strong job-related evidence, and material job-related warning signs. Include a probe separating
individual contribution from team outcome when the selected evidence describes collective work.

When evaluation guidance is requested, define `Score 1:`, `Score 3:`, and `Score 5:` using observable
answer evidence. Never score protected traits, style affinity, confidence, school prestige, gaps in
employment, or keyword counts. Anchors support consistent evidence gathering; they do not produce a
final verdict.

## Timeboxing and fairness

Allow enough time for answers and probes. If requested question volume does not fit, extend the
timebox transparently or mark excess questions as alternates. Apply every protected-trait exclusion
and legal-source boundary in `hiring-integrity.md`. Finish with a coverage, timing, privacy,
fairness, and human-decision-ownership check.
