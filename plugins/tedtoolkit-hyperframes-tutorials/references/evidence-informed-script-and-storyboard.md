# Evidence-informed script and storyboard decisions

Use this reference when designing or reviewing a narrated lesson. The linked evidence supports choices about conversational language, attention, practice, and coordinated audio and visuals. It does not prove that a particular joke, metaphor, or video will improve this course. Check the result with the intended learners and the lesson's observable outcome.
These findings guide the explanation; they do not require a restrained visual style or make every
animation justify itself as a separate learning intervention. Illustration, expressive movement,
humor, and visual surprise can give a lesson personality when its essential information remains clear.
